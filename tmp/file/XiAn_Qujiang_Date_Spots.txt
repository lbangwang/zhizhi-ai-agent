📍 10 Romantic & Date-Friendly Locations Within 10 km of Xi'an Qujiang District

1. 🌸 Qujiang Pool Ruins Park (曲江池遗址公园)
   • Free, 24-hour open, metro-accessible.
   • Lakeside pavilions, willow-lined paths, lotus ponds — serene & photogenic. Ideal for sunset strolls.

2. ❤️ Han Yao Ruins Park (曲江寒窑遗址公园)
   • Home of the legendary Wang Baochuan & Xue Pinggui love story.
   • Ancient kiln caves, poetic inscriptions, tranquil courtyards — deeply symbolic & intimate.

3. 🏯 Da Ci’en Temple Ruins Park (大慈恩寺遗址公园)
   • Surrounds the iconic Big Wild Goose Pagoda.
   • Classical gardens, stone inscriptions, quiet meditation zones — spiritual & timeless ambiance.

4. 🏰 Tang Furong Garden (大唐芙蓉园)
   • 5A-rated Tang Dynasty theme park — night light shows, boat rides, imperial-style architecture.
   • Perfect for immersive, memorable first or milestone dates.

5. 🛡️ Tang City Wall Ruins Park (唐城墙遗址公园)
   • Elevated greenway atop reconstructed Tang ramparts.
   • Panoramic city views, shaded walking paths, artistic installations — peaceful & elevated perspective.

6. ⚔️ Qin Er Shi Mausoleum Ruins Park (秦二世陵遗址公园)
   • Ancient imperial tomb site with forested trails & historical markers.
   • Quiet, contemplative, off-the-beaten-path — great for deep conversation.

7. 🎨 Qujiang Creative Valley (曲江创意谷)
   • Trendy arts district with boutique cafés, indie bookshops, street art & rooftop bars.
   • Young, vibrant energy — ideal for coffee dates, gallery hopping, or casual hangs.

8. 🎮 Qujiang Esports Culture Park (曲江电竞文化公园)
   • Modern park with digital art installations, gaming zones, open plazas & LED landscapes.
   • Fun, interactive, and energetic — perfect for couples sharing tech/gaming interests.

9. 🌉 Daci'en Temple North Square (大雁塔北广场)
   • Grand fountain-music show (evenings), illuminated pagoda backdrop, spacious plaza.
   • Iconic, lively, and romantic — especially during seasonal light festivals.

10. 🌿 Qujiang Ecological Park (曲江生态公园)
    • Expansive lakes, wetlands, cycling paths & bird-watching towers.
    • Natural serenity away from crowds — ideal for relaxed picnics or quiet bike rides together.

💡 Pro Tips:
• All locations are within Qujiang District — no travel beyond ~10 km from any residential area there.
• Public transport (Metro Lines 3, 4, 5) connects most sites directly.
• Best times: Early morning (peaceful), golden hour (photogenic), or evening (light shows, café ambiance).
• For dining: Try 'Yun Shang' (rooftop lake view) near Qujiang Pool, or 'Furong Bieyuan' (Tang-style courtyard restaurant).
