【西安曲江5公里浪漫约会计划】

Location: Xi'an Qujiang New Area (centered on Qujiang Pool Ruins Park; fully walkable + short bus rides)
Best Season & Time: Spring/Autumn, 4:00 PM – 10:00 PM (avoid midday heat or winter chill)
Transport Tip: Metro Line 4 "Datang Furongyuan" Station + Buses 21/22/601 cover all venues

Five-Stage Romantic Itinerary (approx. 6 hours):

Stage 1: 4:00–5:30 PM — Serene Opening at Qujiang Pool Ruins Park
   • Stroll along willow-lined lakeside paths: "Yanbo Island" and "Qujiang Flowing Wine" stele pavilion
   • Must-see spot: Ziyun Tower viewing platform (free entry; panoramic sunset view over the lake)
   • Tips: Free admission; bring a picnic mat for lakeside relaxation; golden-hour lighting is ideal for photos
   Photo suggestion: Sunset over Qujiang Pool — golden light reflecting on water, willow silhouettes

Stage 2: 5:45–6:45 PM — Artistic Interlude at Qujiang Creative Valley
   • Walk 8 min or take Bus 22 for 1 stop → arrive at the "Red Brick Art District"
   • Recommended stops: M Cafe (floor-to-ceiling windows + Tang-style courtyard), Librairie Avant-Garde bookstore (craft corner + coffee nook)
   • Tips: Order osmanthus oolong tea, browse poetry collections, capture retro-style street photos
   Photo suggestion: Red-brick alley with mural art and hanging lanterns — soft afternoon light

Stage 3: 7:00–8:30 PM — Authentic Dinner at Chang'an Grand Restaurant (Da Tang All Night Town branch)
   • 5-min walk from Stage 2; book 1 day ahead via Dianping app (request "2nd-floor street-view private booth")
   • Must-order: Crispy Chicken (tender & aromatic), Liangpi & Biangbiang Noodles, Persimmon Cake + Osmanthus Yogurt
   • Tips: Warm lantern lighting and carved wooden screens create intimacy; staff are friendly — perfect for first dates
   Photo suggestion: Warm-lit courtyard dining with Tang-style decor and steaming dishes

Stage 4: 8:45–10:15 PM — Grand Night Tour of Da Tang All Night Town
   • Step out directly into the district; recommended route: Kaiyuan Square → North Square Fountain → "Unstoppable Lady" interactive zone → "Shengtang Secret Box" live theater
   • Tips: Light show begins nightly at 8:00 PM; wear light-colored or Tang-inspired attire for best photos; check street performer schedules
   Photo suggestion: Nighttime panoramic view of Da Tang All Night Town — glowing towers, flowing crowds, fountain lights

Stage 5: 10:30–11:00 PM — Gentle Closing at Qujiang Book City Rooftop Café
   • 3-min walk; head to 3rd-floor terrace, order two glasses of mulled wine, enjoy distant views of Big Wild Goose Pagoda under starlight
   • Tips: Open until 11:30 PM; quiet ambiance + string lights = perfect ending moment
   Photo suggestion: Rooftop view at night — quiet intimacy, warm lights, distant silhouette of Big Wild Goose Pagoda

Bonus Tips:
• Rainy-day plan: Replace Stages 1 & 2 with Xi'an Art Museum (free entry; permanent exhibitions + glass-domed café)
• Souvenir idea: Buy a pair of ceramic keychains from "Tang妞"文创 store in Qujiang Creative Valley
• Safety reminder: Keep belongings secure at night; use "Xi'an Metro" app for seamless QR-code boarding

Heartfelt Note: Qujiang’s blend of Tang dynasty elegance and modern charm mirrors your relationship — timeless yet vibrant, calm yet full of life. Wishing you a truly unforgettable date!
